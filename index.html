Mission

Mission statement

An archive of women’s lives, told in their own words.

This project archives women’s life and experience through interviews. Our goal is to document everyday stories (holidays, family, work, hometown) and pass them on to the next generation.

Purpose

Why take part

Your life story, preserved as a lasting record.

By joining an interview, your experiences and memories are kept in an organised form, a legacy that lasts beyond any one lifetime. Reflecting on and organising your life along the way can also bring new perspectives and deeper meaning.

Our story

Our Story

LivingArchive started with a simple realisation: the stories we heard at the kitchen table were disappearing. Recipes passed down by hand, the way a holiday used to be kept, what work was like for a woman decades ago. Most of it was never written anywhere.

So we began asking. We sit down with women, listen, and record their answers carefully. Each conversation is transcribed, reviewed with the person who told it, and added to this archive with her permission.

We are a small, student-led team. Every interview we add is one more life that the next generation can still hear from.

— The LivingArchive team

Participate

How an interview works

  1. Get in touch

    Message us by email or Instagram. You can take part yourself or introduce a mother, grandmother or neighbour.

  2. Sit down and talk

    We meet at a time and place that suits you. The conversation usually takes an hour or two, and you choose what to share.

  3. Review your words

    We send you the transcript. You can correct, add or remove anything before it is published.

  4. Join the archive

    Your interview is catalogued with its own record number and kept here for the next generation.
